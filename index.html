That Human Touch

I’ve been experimenting with AI tools to help bring my lyrics and ideas to life. These demos are a way for me to explore what a song can become when an idea moves from paper into sound. Someday, I’d love to hear some of them interpreted by real musicians and discover where they could go with a human touch. If any of these songs spark an idea, let's connect.
